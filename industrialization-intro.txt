Hello and good day everyone out there, here is another episode of Poetic Codes. Today we ask a question Filipinos have debated for generations: why did the Philippines, once one of the most promising economies in Asia, never industrialize the way Japan, Korea, Indonesia, and Vietnam did? We will look at colonial dependence, weak state capacity, protection without discipline, land inequality, and labor export, and at what the country can still learn from its neighbors. Let's begin.
