Last updated: November 12, 2026

Poetic Codes is an independent publication of long-form essays on Philippine history, food and culture, politics, philosophy, technology and music. It is written and run by one person, Glenn Junsay Pansensoy, from the Philippines. If you have read something here and want to respond, I would like to hear from you.

Email

The best way to reach me is by email: pansensoyglenn150@gmail.com

This is a one-person site, so replies can take a few days. I read every message.

What to write to me about

  • Corrections. If you find a factual error in an essay, tell me the title, the sentence and, if you can, a source. I fix verified errors and note significant changes on the page.
  • Permission to quote or republish. Short quotations with a link back to the essay are welcome. For longer excerpts or republishing, ask first.
  • Privacy requests. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Accessibility problems. See the Accessibility statement.
  • Topic suggestions and general questions about the site.

Elsewhere
